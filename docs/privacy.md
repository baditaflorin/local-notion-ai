# Privacy

`local-notion-ai` does not collect analytics in v1.

Documents are imported, indexed, summarized, rewritten, and queried inside your browser. The app does not upload document contents to a server.

Local data is stored in your browser's IndexedDB. Clearing browser site data can delete your workspace. Use the export feature to keep your own backup.
